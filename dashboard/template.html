Metric contract

  • Purchase cohort: 1 January 2017 to 30 June 2018 inclusive. Final recorded outcomes are analysed.
  • One row per order. Items are aggregated and one latest valid review is selected before joining.
  • Eligible delivery: recorded status delivered, known purchase/delivery/promise timestamps, valid purchase-to-delivery and promise chronology.
  • On time: delivered calendar date is on or before the estimated delivery date. Same-day delivery after midnight remains on time.
  • P90: inclusive linear-interpolated 90th percentile of eligible order elapsed delivery seconds / 86,400.
  • Low score: latest valid score 1–2, with a reviewed-eligible-order denominator.
  • Stage comparisons use records with purchase ≤ handover ≤ delivery. Both stages share that denominator.
  • Rate intervals are Wilson 95% reference intervals. They do not control for route/product mix or clustered sellers.

Scope and evidence

Olist published anonymised Brazilian marketplace orders from 2016–2018. This earlier purchase cohort reduces exposure to the dataset's tail; it cannot establish current service performance.

The data does not supply a reliable carrier identity, damage/loss events, implemented interventions or an observation date for reconstructing a live backlog.

Multi-seller orders remain in overall delivery metrics and are excluded from single-seller state-pair comparisons. Categories containing several product categories are labelled explicitly.

No customer identifiers, source order identifiers, city/postcode coordinates or review comments are embedded here. Case codes are analytical references.
